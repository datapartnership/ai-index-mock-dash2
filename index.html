ⓘ Noise, sampling and disclosure

A published Index would add calibrated noise to aggregated consumer-activity and API-volume values before assigning countries to deciles. Deciles show broad groups, not precise ranks. Small samples and sensitive cross-tabulations may be suppressed. This mock-up uses invented values and does not implement the production noise or suppression methods.
