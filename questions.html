Global AI Adoption Index

Stakeholder Questions

The World Bank conducted a global expert survey of 164 development practitioners and led focus groups and interviews with more than 70 experts from international organizations, including the African Development Bank, Inter-American Development Bank, UNICEF, OECD, and others. Based on these inputs, the Bank has compiled 222 test questions for the Index to answer related to AI policy and investment prioritization. The eight illustrative examples below use a fictional country to combine Index outputs with supplemental datasets.
